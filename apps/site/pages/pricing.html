Pricing

Free where it is local. A plan where it is hosted.

Working alone and on your own network is free and never stopped by a limit. Hosted sessions through the relay need a plan.

Prices are not decided yet. The limits below are the current plan settings; the server is the final word and they may change before launch.

Good to know

  • Limits are enforced by the server. The apps show your plan and warn before you reach a limit.
  • "Unlimited" means the plan sets no cap on that item.
  • Bring your own Claude Code or Codex: their usage is billed by their providers, not by Centcom.
