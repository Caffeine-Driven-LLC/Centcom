# Accounts and plans

Working alone and on your own network is free and needs no account. Hosted sessions through the relay need an account on a plan with relay access. `centcom login` signs in, and `centcom whoami` shows who you are, your plan and your workspace.

## Plans

The server decides what each plan allows; Centcom only shows the limits and warns before you reach one. The reference values of the contract are below, and they can change without a new release of Centcom.

| Limit | Free | Pro | Team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relay access | no | yes | yes |
| Seats | 1 | 1 | 5, with add-on seats |
| People in a session | 8 (LAN) | 4 | 12 |
| Hosted sessions at once | 0 | 2 | 10 |
| Parallel agents | 4 | 8 | 16 |
| Session history | none | 7 days | 30 days |
| Hosted minutes a month | 0 | 6,000 | 30,000, pooled |
| Audit log | none | none | 90 days |
| Webhooks | 0 | 2 | 20 |
| API keys | 1 | 5 | 50 |

## When you reach a limit

At 80% of a metered limit you get a warning. At 100% new approvals and new agents in hosted sessions pause and running work finishes. **LAN and local use are never blocked.** A payment problem keeps your plan for seven days, and a cancelled plan stays until the end of the period it was paid for.
