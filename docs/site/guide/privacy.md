# Privacy and encryption

What your team sends through the relay is encrypted on your computer with keys the relay never has.

<!--@include: ./privacy-relay.md-->

See the [environment variables](../reference/env.md) for turning telemetry off (`DO_NOT_TRACK=1`, `CENTCOM_TELEMETRY=off`). Telemetry is off unless you turn it on, and never contains message text, paths or names.
