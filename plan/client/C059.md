## Goal
Send and receive presence, typing and cursor frames with the rates and lifetimes the contract fixes, and expose a simple roster-presence model to the UI, without ever depending on presence being delivered.

## In scope
- `presence.update {status:'online'|'away'|'busy', activity:'idle'|'typing'|'reviewing'|'running', agent_count?}` sender: emitted on change and at most once per second, coalescing to the latest value.
- Auto-away after 5 minutes without input (injected input-activity source), user-chosen `busy`, typing indicator refresh while typing (re-send every 3 s so the relay's 5 s auto-clear does not fire).
- `presence.cursor` (encrypted `{path?,line?,col?,sel_end_line?,sel_end_col?}`): sender limited to 10/s per member, latest-wins, only when the cursor actually moved.
- Receiver model: per-member presence map seeded by the post-welcome presence burst; cursors expire locally after 10 s without update; offline members shown offline when `control.member_left` or the connection state says so (10 s server grace respected by not flapping).
- Frames sent as ephemeral `t:'presence'` via ReliableChannel.sendEphemeral (never sequenced, never resent).

## Out of scope
- Rendering cursors/avatars: lane C077 (TUI) and C085 (web). Slot-to-colour mapping: presentation lanes (design system).
- Member roster ownership and key handling: lane C057.
- Reliability, ordering and acks: lane C055 (presence is excluded from them).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Calling `setActivity('typing')` 50 times in 1 s sends at most 1 `presence.update` frame in that second, and the last call's value is the one on the wire.
2. While typing continues, a refresh frame is sent every 3 s (fake clock); 5 s after the last keypress `setActivity('idle')` is sent.
3. With no input for 300 s (fake clock) `status:'away'` is sent once; the next input sends `online` once; `busy` set by the user is not overwritten by auto-away.
4. `setCursor` called 100 times in 1 s sends at most 10 `presence.cursor` frames; each has no `p` and a `ct` that decrypts to the cursor object; identical consecutive positions send nothing.
5. A received cursor disappears from `members()` 10 s after its last update (fake clock); a presence update replaces the previous one for the same member.
6. After welcome the model is populated from the burst (one frame per member) without sending anything itself.
7. No presence or cursor frame is ever present in the ReliableChannel outbox after a forced disconnect, and none is resent after reconnect.
8. If presence frames are never delivered (simulator drops all), session send/receive, queue and approvals continue unaffected (no awaited presence promise anywhere).
9. Frames from a non-roster `from` are ignored; a cursor whose `ct` fails verification is dropped silently with a counter.

## Tests required
- presence/throttle.test.ts: 1/s update limit, 10/s cursor limit, typing refresh cadence on a fake clock.
- presence/model.test.ts: burst seeding, expiry at 10 s, away/busy rules.
- presence/client.test.ts: two clients through the simulator, cursor ct round trip.
- presence/ephemeral.test.ts: no buffering or resend across reconnect.
- presence/privacy.test.ts: cursor path/line only inside ct; update p holds enums only.

## Guardrails (MUST / MUST NOT)
- MUST send `presence.cursor` only encrypted (path/line/col are work content); MUST NOT put any of them in cleartext `p`.
- MUST NOT buffer, resend or sequence presence frames and MUST NOT make any feature wait on presence delivery (best-effort per CT-WS-PRESENCE).
- MUST honour the sender limits (1/s updates, 10/s cursors) regardless of caller behaviour; excess calls coalesce, they are not queued.
- MUST NOT treat presence as proof of identity or authority; roles come from the roster/server.
- MUST stop sending presence while the connection is not `ready` and while the member is muted; viewers MAY send `presence.update` and `presence.cursor` (CT-RBAC session-action table).

## Failure modes
- Server sends `sys.slow_down` -> presence is dropped first (coalesce to latest), sequenced frames keep priority.
- Clock jump or suspended laptop -> on resume the stale cursors expire at once and a single fresh `presence.update` is sent; no burst of backlog.
- Unknown `status`/`activity` value received -> treated as 'online'/'idle' for display and logged at debug.
