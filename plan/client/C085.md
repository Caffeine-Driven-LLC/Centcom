## Goal
Web users see who is in the session, what they are doing and where their cursors are, with identity never carried by colour alone and presence treated strictly as best-effort.

## In scope
- Roster in apps/web/src/presence/: presence stack in the header (<= 5 avatars then `+N`, 32 px square, -8 px overlap), roster popover with avatar, name, role chip (HOST/EDIT/VIEW), colour name, activity ('typing', 'reviewing diff', 'away 4m'), nudge button.
- Presence publisher: `presence.update {status, activity, agent_count?}` on change at most once per second; `away` after 5 min without input; typing debounce 400 ms; `busy` user-chosen.
- Cursor layer: render remote `presence.cursor` (encrypted) as 4x6 pixel arrows with name tags over code/diff views, tags fade to 40 % after 3 s idle and vanish after 10 s; own cursor unlabelled.
- Slot to colour mapping and join/leave toasts ('Maya joined'), avatar enter `base` 160 ms/`enter` and leave `slow` 280 ms/`exit`.
- Roster state from `control.roster`, `control.member_joined`, `control.member_left`, `control.role`, `control.host_changed`.

## Out of scope
- TUI cursors (C077) and presence client in packages/net (C059).
- Transcript, fleet board (C083, C084).
- Role changes/kick UI actions beyond showing the chips (handled with C083 host controls).
- Read receipts (off by default, not built).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Presence dot colours: online `status.success`, away `status.warning`, offline `border.strong`, busy `status.danger`, each with a distinct glyph shape (check, clock, dash, minus) and an accessible name ('Maya, online').
2. Initial text is white on violet and brown, ink-900 on red, yellow, green (DESIGN.md 4.6); brown avatars have the 1 px abyss-300 ring; contrast test passes >= 3:1 on every pair.
3. Publisher never exceeds 1 presence.update/s and 10 presence.cursor/s under a synthetic 100 events/s input; typing indicator clears 5 s after the last keystroke.
4. A remote cursor idle 3 s drops to 40 % opacity and is removed at 10 s; on member_left it is removed immediately.
5. Sixth participant renders violet with an outline and a distinct initial; roster text reads 'M · Maya · green'.
6. Roster popover is a focus-trapped dialog with Esc to close and returns focus to the trigger; stack has `aria-label="Participants, N online"`.
7. Join and leave announce via aria-live polite ('Maya joined'); toasts with no action auto-dismiss at 4 s; no solo-session typing indicators.

## Guardrails (MUST / MUST NOT)
- MUST NOT make any feature depend on a presence frame being delivered (best-effort).
- MUST NOT send cursor path or text in `p`; cursor payload is encrypted only.
- Nudges: rate limit one per person per minute client-side, always dismissible, and sent with existing frames only (see notes).
- MUST NOT identify people by colour alone; name + initial always present (DESIGN.md 16.3).

## Failure modes
- Presence frame for unknown member -> hold up to 10 s for roster, then drop.
- Roster version gap (control.roster.version jumps) -> trust the newest full roster; discard partials.
- Clock skew -> use local receive time for fade/expiry, never frame `ts`.

## Definition of done
Everything in [GUIDELINES §8](../GUIDELINES.md) plus every acceptance item above covered by a test.
