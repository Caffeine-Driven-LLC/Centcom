## Goal
Provide ephemeral presence: track each member's `presence.update`, coalesce to the latest per member, fan out at most once per 500 ms, give joiners a snapshot right after `welcome`, and derive online/offline from the socket with a 10 s grace.

## In scope
- Presence stage for `t:'presence'`, `k:'presence.update'` (clear `p`: `status` online|away|busy, `activity` idle|typing|reviewing|running, `agent_count?`).
- Inbound limit: at most 1 update/s per member (extra ones overwrite the pending value, no error); server coalesces to the latest and fans out at most once per 500 ms per member.
- Redis hash `presence:{sid}` with per-member field and key TTL (60 s, refreshed on any activity) for multi-node reads; in-memory implementation for tests.
- Presence snapshot: one coalesced frame per member sent to a connection right after `welcome`.
- Connection-derived online/offline: `isOnline(mid)`; 10 s grace after the last socket of a member closes before the member is considered offline.
- Never sequenced, never replayed, never written to the durable log; sent via ephemeral channel (droppable).

## Out of scope
- Cursor and typing throttling/expiry (B048).
- `control.member_left` and `control.member_joined` emission (control lanes).
- Presence history / last-seen API (B067).

## Build against (no counterpart needed)
The mock client simulator from B011 (scripted fake clients speaking the WS protocol), the in-memory B009 KV/pub-sub implementations, and the frame fixtures in contracts/fixtures/events/; no real client is needed. Fake clock for the 500 ms, 1 s and 10 s timers; in-memory presence store; cluster tests use the in-memory pub/sub only.

## Interfaces (the only surface other lanes may use)
- TS: `interface PresenceService { update(sid: string, mid: string, p: PresenceUpdate, nowMs: number): void; snapshot(sid: string): Array<{ member: string; p: PresenceUpdate }>; isOnline(sid: string, mid: string): boolean; onConnect(sid: string, mid: string): void; onDisconnect(sid: string, mid: string): void }`
- TS: `type PresenceUpdate = { status: 'online' | 'away' | 'busy'; activity: 'idle' | 'typing' | 'reviewing' | 'running'; agent_count?: number }`
- TS: `export default relayModule: RelayModule` (order 35) registering the presence stage and the post-welcome snapshot hook.
- Redis: `presence:{sid}` hash, TTL 60 s.
- Env: `RELAY_PRESENCE_IN_MS` (1000), `RELAY_PRESENCE_OUT_MS` (500), `RELAY_OFFLINE_GRACE_MS` (10000).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A member sending 10 `presence.update` frames in 1 s results in at most 2 fan-out frames for that member in that second, the last carrying the final value.
2. After `welcome`, a joiner receives exactly one presence frame per member that has published one, before any live presence updates.
3. Presence frames have no `seq`, are absent from the hot buffer and from replay, and do not change `head` (assert on SeqStore).
4. A member whose only socket closes stays online for 10 s (+-0.5 s); a reconnect inside the grace never marks them offline; after the grace `isOnline` is false.
5. Invalid payload (`status: 'busy2'`) yields `sys.error` `invalid_frame` and does not alter stored state; unknown extra fields in `p` are dropped.
6. A viewer may send `presence.update` (allowed for every role); a client-supplied `from` is ignored and the member id is stamped.
7. Presence entries expire from Redis after 60 s without activity, so a crashed node leaves no permanent ghost.
8. 1 000 members' updates across 20 sessions cost < 10 % CPU of one core in the benchmark.

## Tests required
- presence.coalesce.test.ts: in/out rates with fake clock (property test).
- presence.snapshot.test.ts: ordering after welcome.
- presence.not-sequenced.test.ts: seq, buffer and durable log untouched.
- presence.offline-grace.test.ts: reconnect inside/outside grace.
- presence.ttl.test.ts: Redis expiry.
- presence.validation.test.ts: enums, unknown fields.

## Guardrails (MUST / MUST NOT)
- Presence MUST NOT be sequenced, buffered, replayed or stored durably (CT-WS-PRESENCE); no feature may depend on its delivery.
- Presence frames MUST be the lowest-priority traffic: droppable under backpressure (B046).
- The relay MUST derive online/offline from the connection, never from a client-claimed value.
- Only `status`, `activity` and `agent_count` are read; no other field is stored or logged.
- Per-member state MUST be bounded (one entry per member, max 50 per session).

## Failure modes
- Redis unavailable -> presence falls back to node-local memory and logs once; sequenced traffic unaffected.
- Member state for an ended session -> removed at session end and by TTL.
- Flood of updates -> overwritten in place, never queued.
