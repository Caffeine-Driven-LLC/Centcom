## Goal
Protect the relay and healthy clients from slow consumers and floods by enforcing the 2 MiB per-connection outbound limit with `sys.slow_down`, then close 4429, while never silently dropping sequenced frames.

## In scope
- Outbound buffer accounting per connection (`ws.bufferedAmount` plus the B044 send queue bytes).
- Policy: above 1 MiB (50 % watermark) drop `droppable` ephemeral frames (presence, cursor) for that connection; above 2 MiB send `sys.slow_down {for_ms: 2000}` once, start a 5 s grace timer; if the buffer is not below 1 MiB within the grace, close 4429 after a `sys.error`.
- Rate of `sys.slow_down` frames limited to one per second per connection.
- Send-queue pause/resume hooks so replay (B042) waits for drain.
- Per-node global memory guard: if total buffered bytes exceed `RELAY_NODE_BUFFER_MAX` (default 1 GiB), close the largest buffers first with 4429 and stop accepting new connections (readiness degraded).
- Metrics: buffered bytes histogram, slow_down count, 4429 closes.

## Out of scope
- Inbound sequenced-frame rate limit (B041).
- Heartbeat (B040), fan-out ordering (B044).
- Client-side behaviour on `sys.slow_down`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A client that stops reading while 100 KiB frames are sent receives `sys.slow_down` once the buffer passes 2 MiB (+-100 KiB) and is closed 4429 at 5 s (+-0.5 s) if it did not drain below 1 MiB.
2. If the client resumes reading within the grace and the buffer falls under 1 MiB, no close occurs and delivery continues in order with no frame lost.
3. Presence/cursor frames to a connection above 1 MiB are dropped (counter increments) while sequenced frames are still queued in order.
4. At most one `sys.slow_down` per second per connection even when 1 000 frames are enqueued.
5. Replay of 5 000 frames to a slow reader never lets buffered bytes exceed 2 MiB + one frame (256 KiB).
6. Other connections in the same room keep a delivery latency p95 within 2x baseline while one consumer is stalled.
7. A closed-for-4429 client reconnecting with `last_seq` resumes without loss (end-to-end with B042 in the integration test).
8. Node-level guard: forcing total buffered bytes above `RELAY_NODE_BUFFER_MAX` closes the largest buffers first until below 90 %, and `/readyz` reports `checks.buffers.ok=false` while above 100 %.

## Guardrails (MUST / MUST NOT)
- Sequenced frames MUST NEVER be dropped to relieve pressure; the only remedies are slowing the producer and closing the slow consumer (it recovers by resume).
- Every buffer in the relay MUST have a documented cap; no queue may be unbounded (GUIDELINES section 3.7).
- `sys.slow_down` MUST NOT itself go through the congested queue (write directly) and MUST NOT be sent more than once per second.
- Ephemeral frames MUST be droppable and never retried or replayed.
- Backpressure decisions MUST NOT depend on frame content; sizes only.

## Failure modes
- Socket `bufferedAmount` unavailable -> fall back to app-side byte counting; log once.
- Many connections hit the cap at once (network event) -> closes are spread by 0-500 ms jitter to avoid a reconnect storm.
- Timer leak on close -> all grace timers are cleared in `onClose`.
