## Goal
Let a reconnecting client resume: replay missed sequenced frames from the hot buffer (or the durable log after a snapshot), report the window with `sys.resumed`, or tell the client a snapshot is required.

## In scope
- Resume handling for `sys.hello.last_seq` and the standalone `sys.resume {last_seq}` frame.
- Replay from the hot buffer in `seq` order in batches of 100 frames with a send-queue limit, then `sys.resumed {from_seq, to_seq, count}`; `sys.resumed {snapshot_required:true, snapshot_seq}` when `last_seq` is older than the buffer.
- Replay after snapshot: `sys.resume {last_seq: snapshot_seq}` served from the durable log (via a `DurableLogReader` port implemented on top of B055) when the hot buffer no longer has those frames.
- Hot-buffer rehydration from the durable log after a relay/Redis restart so `head` and recent frames are available.
- Live-frame handoff: while a connection is replaying, new live frames are held in a bounded per-connection queue and released after the replay in order, with no gap and no duplicate.
- Wiring of the `DurableAppend` port from B041 to the B055 store.

## Out of scope
- Sequencing/hot buffer itself (B041), durable history storage and REST history endpoint (B055), snapshots (B056).
- Fan-out of live frames (B044).
- Client-side decrypt and snapshot application.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Client disconnects at seq 1040 while head moves to 1100; reconnecting with `last_seq: 1040` receives welcome, then frames 1041..1100 exactly once in order, then `sys.resumed {from_seq:1041,to_seq:1100,count:60}`; frames 1101+ arrive after, with no duplicate or gap (checked over 20 runs with traffic during replay).
2. `last_seq` equal to head -> `sys.resumed {count:0}` and no frames; `last_seq: null` -> no replay and `welcome.resume = null`.
3. `last_seq` older than the oldest buffered frame -> `sys.resumed {snapshot_required:true, snapshot_seq}` and no replay frames; `last_seq` greater than head -> same snapshot_required answer.
4. After snapshot_required, `sys.resume {last_seq: S}` replays S+1..head from the durable log in order and ends with a correct `sys.resumed`.
5. Replay of 5 000 frames completes within 2 s on the test container and never lets the connection's outbound buffer exceed 2 MiB (batching waits for drain).
6. A replay request spanning more than `RELAY_REPLAY_MAX_FRAMES` (50 000) is answered with snapshot_required.
7. A member whose membership was revoked between disconnect and reconnect gets 4403 from the handshake and receives no replay.
8. After a simulated Redis flush, the first connection triggers hydration; `head` equals the durable log's max seq and the next sequenced frame is head+1, not 1.

## Guardrails (MUST / MUST NOT)
- Replayed frames MUST be byte-identical to the originals (including `seq`, `ts`, `ct`, `sig`); the relay MUST NOT re-encrypt, decrypt or reformat content.
- Replay MUST be authorised by the live membership check at resume time and MUST NOT cross sessions (`sid` of the ticket only).
- Replay MUST be ordered and gapless relative to live frames; a gap or duplicate after resume is a correctness bug, not a tolerated race.
- Replay MUST honour outbound buffer limits (B046); never push an unbounded burst.
- After a data loss the relay MUST NOT restart `seq` from 1 for an existing session; `head` is recovered from the durable log or the session is paused.

## Failure modes
- Durable log unavailable during snapshot-based resume -> `sys.error` 503-class with `retry_after_s`, connection kept; client retries.
- Hydration fails -> session sequencing paused (503-class) and alert metric `relay_hydrate_failed_total`; not silently reset.
- Client sends `sys.resume` twice concurrently -> second is ignored with `sys.error` `invalid_frame`.
