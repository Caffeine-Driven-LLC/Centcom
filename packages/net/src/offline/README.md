# offline behaviour (lane C063)

- **`NetStateMonitor`**: one answer from the session sockets and the status feed (`GET /v1/status`, every 15 s): `online`, `reconnecting` (first close), `offline` (second failed attempt, a socket that gave up, or the service unreachable twice), `degraded` (partial outage: shown, nothing is closed), `backend-down` (server errors). `toMascotState` gives `online | reconnecting | offline` for the mascot and status line.
- **Durable outbox** (`createDurableOutbox(dir, sid, { keychain })`): pass it to `createSession` / `joinSession` as `outbox`. While the socket is down, `sendEvent` keeps the frame on disk (500 frames and 4 MiB at most, `OfflineBufferFull` beyond that, nothing dropped) and returns at once with `seq: 0`; after the reconnect the frames are sent in order, each with its original id (so the relay's de-duplication makes a replay safe) and encrypted then, under the key of that moment. The file holds one encrypted line per frame, the key lives in the OS keychain; a torn or damaged line is skipped with a warning. Write a queue size as `"$ctBytes"` so it can be filled in when the frame is encrypted.
- **`createFileSeqStore(dir)`**: the position in each session, kept across restarts: the next hello carries it and the relay replays only what came after.
- **`ResumeCoordinator(session)`**: reports `restored` (snapshot) and `history-incomplete` (once). The session client does the work: hot resume, snapshot, REST history.
- **`WakeDetector`**: a gap of over 30 s between ticks means the sockets are probably dead: every target reconnects (fresh ticket each) after 0 to 2 s of jitter.
- **`requireOnline(fn, { state?, timeoutMs? })`**: hosted-only actions fail with `OfflineError` at once when the state is offline or backend-down, and after a second when they do not answer. LAN and local sessions never use it. Cached entitlements are fine to show for 24 h (`cachedForDisplay`) and never decide a hosted action.

Not done: a frame buffered offline is sent under the key current when the link comes back; if a `control.rotate_key` is still on its way in the replay, that frame goes out under the older key (the new epoch's key may not have arrived yet). The mock has no pause/restart, so "killed for 60 s" is simulated with its maintenance mode.
